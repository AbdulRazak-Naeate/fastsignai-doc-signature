Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

SignFast AI ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SignFast AI mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

By installing and using SignFast AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Document and Signature Data (Local Processing)

SignFast AI is designed as a local device utility.

  • No Server Uploads: All document ingestion (PDFs, images), vector signature drawing, drag-and-drop coordinate mapping, and file flattening operations occur strictly within your mobile device's sandbox memory.
  • No Collection: We do not collect, view, store, or transmit the contents of your documents, contracts, signatures, or initials to any external servers or third parties.

B. Automatically Collected Information via Third-Party Services

Certain third-party services used by the App may automatically collect limited technical and advertising-related information, which may include:

  • Your device's IP address.
  • Unique mobile device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA, where applicable.
  • Device operating system version, model, and hardware settings.
  • Aggregated, anonymous interaction data, such as ad impressions, clicks, and app launch logs.

2. How Information is Used

The limited data collected by our third-party advertising partner, Google AdMob, is used solely to:

  • Deliver, manage, and optimize digital advertisements within the App.
  • Provide basic, anonymous app analytics to help us monitor technical app stability, crashes, and performance optimizations.

3. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

Because SignFast AI does not utilize remote cloud storage databases, we do not store your documents or signatures on our servers.

  • User Control: Your documents are saved exclusively in the local directory space you choose on your device.
  • Data Deletion: If you wish to delete your documents or signature history from the App, you can clear the App's cache through your device settings or uninstall the App, where applicable.
  • Security: While we implement industry-standard local security measures to safeguard your App environment, the ultimate security of your signed documents depends on your device's security, including screen locks and file encryption.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal document data from any user, we do not maintain data structures belonging to children.

If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child's device has provided information to our third-party ad networks, please contact us so we can request its removal from those networks.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
